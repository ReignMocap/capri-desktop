Capri on your desktop  (work in progress)
=========================================

Capri, the hologram from Voxta for Cyberpunk, as a little companion on your Windows desktop.
She stands on your taskbar, talks with you through Voxta in her own voice, lip syncs, glitches,
and zips around the screen in a crack of lightning.

This is an early version: things may change or break.


What you need
-------------
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- Voxta 1.13.1 or newer, running on this PC (voxta.ai).
- In Voxta: a text generation service, and a text-to-speech service for her voice.
  Her character uses Voxta Cloud's "Cappi" voice.
- To talk to her out loud: speech-to-text and your microphone set up in Voxta.


Start her
---------
1. Start Voxta.
2. Unzip this folder anywhere you like (not inside Program Files).
3. Double-click CapriDesktop.exe.

The first time, she installs her own character into Voxta (Capri), the same one the Cyberpunk mod
uses. If you already have it, she leaves it as it is.


Using her
---------
- Talk out loud, or click her to open the type box (Esc closes it).
- Drag her anywhere. The mouse wheel over her makes her bigger or smaller.
- Right-click her for the menu: her look (full body or face HUD), poses, mic, voice, volume,
  Settings and Quit.
- Settings: how often she zips about, her size when she lands, how see-through she is, her
  sounds, her form, and Voxta's address and API key.


If something's wrong
--------------------
- "Can't reach Voxta": start Voxta, or set its address in Settings.
- Your Voxta has a password: put an API key from Voxta in Settings, then start her again.
- She doesn't hear you: check the microphone and speech-to-text in Voxta's settings.
- Her log is in %AppData%\CapriDesktop\log.txt.


Remove her
----------
Quit her (right-click, Quit), delete this folder, and delete %AppData%\CapriDesktop.
Her character stays in Voxta; delete it there if you want it gone too.
