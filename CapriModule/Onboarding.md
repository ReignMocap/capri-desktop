# Capri on your desktop

Capri, the hologram from Voxta for Cyberpunk, as a companion on your Windows desktop. Work in progress.

## Start her

1. Open **Play** and press **Play** on Capri. The first time, her app downloads (about 100 MB); then she appears on
   your taskbar.
2. She installs her own character (Capri) into Voxta the first time she connects.

## What she needs

- A text generation service and a text-to-speech service. Her character uses Voxta Cloud's "Cappi" voice.
- To talk to her out loud: speech-to-text and your microphone, set up in Voxta.

## Using her

- Talk out loud, or click her to type (Esc closes the box).
- Drag her anywhere; the mouse wheel over her makes her bigger or smaller.
- Right-click her: her look (full body or face HUD), poses, mic, voice, volume, **Settings** and **Quit**.

Her app is in `%LocalAppData%\Programs\CapriDesktop`, her settings and log in `%AppData%\CapriDesktop`.
